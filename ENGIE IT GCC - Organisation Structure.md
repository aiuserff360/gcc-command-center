# ENGIE IT GCC – organisation structure (from the client, 22 Oct 2026)

ENGIE is a Euro / French-headquartered company setting up an IT GCC in Pune ("ENGIE IT GCC").
Target: 400 Pune IT headcount by Apr 2027, 4 transition waves (Nov 2026 – Apr 2027), supports all ENGIE
businesses (Global, Europe, Americas, AMEA), 7 IT domains. See mockups/09-engie-it-gcc-target-footprint.png.
